Player Metrics & Analytics Guide

Clear explanations of what each number means on the pitch

Coaching Reference
OPR Composite (Tournament MVP)

What it means: The all-around MVP score blending attack, defense, teammate lift, and clutch output.

Look for: Positive is above average; +1.00 is dominant.

Relative ± (Force Multiplier)

What it means: Player's total goal margin minus their rotating teammates' average margin.

Look for: High positive values prove the player lifts whatever squad they join.

RAPM (Adjusted Plus-Minus)

What it means: Pure isolated goal impact removing teammate & opponent strength bias.

Look for: Positive values indicate high isolated talent independent of surroundings.

MWC (Marginal Win Contribution)

What it means: Extra points won above tournament expectation (rewards upsets).

Look for: Identifies difference-makers who win matches their team was slated to lose.

WPA (Win Probability Added)

What it means: Outcome rate: Win = +2, Tie = +1, Loss = -1 divided by games.

Look for: High scores belong to players who protect table points and refuse to lose.

GCR % (Goal Share)

What it means: % of total match goals scored by this player's team.

Look for: 50% is parity; >60% controls the bulk of the action.

GAE (Goals Saved vs. Avg)

What it means: Tournament mean goals conceded minus player's conceded rate.

Look for: Positive values represent lockdown defensive stoppers.

OPR% & DQI%

What it means: OPR% = Games scoring 2+ goals; DQI% = Games allowing ≤1 goal.

Look for: Multi-goal consistency and defensive floor stability.

Clutch Margin

What it means: Goal differential strictly in 1-goal nail-biters.

Look for: Positive values indicate high execution under pressure.

Coaching Notice: This is just a suggestion. Coaches should use their own evaluations to determine players, positions, and lineups.
